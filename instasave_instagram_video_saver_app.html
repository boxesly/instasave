How InstaSave Works

Copy Video Link

Open Instagram app or web, tap on the share menu of any public Reel, IGTV, or post, and click "Copy Link".

02

Paste & Confirm

Paste the copied URL into our downloader above, tick the copyright authorization box, and click "Get Video".

Save Your Video

Select your preferred video resolution or audio format and click Download to save the media file directly to your device.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Is InstaSave free to use?

Yes, InstaSave provides a completely free web interface for processing authorized public media URLs. No hidden subscriptions or account sign-ups are required.

Can I download private Instagram posts?

No. InstaSave strictly processes publicly accessible URLs. Private posts requiring account authentication or permission bypass are not supported.

How do I configure my own RapidAPI downloader backend?

Click on the "API Settings" button at the top header bar. You can enter your custom DOWNLOADER_API_URL and DOWNLOADER_API_KEY. These values will be stored securely in your browser's local storage.

Where are saved files stored on my device?

When you click the "Download Media" button, the video is saved directly to your web browser's default downloads folder (or Photos app on iOS/Android).
